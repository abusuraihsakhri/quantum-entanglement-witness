Interpretation and scope

For a two-qubit state, a negative eigenvalue of ρTB proves entanglement. A non-negative partial transpose establishes separability. The negativity is the sum of the absolute values of negative eigenvalues.

The projective witness W = I/2 − |Bell⟩⟨Bell|, chosen for the largest Bell-state population, has expectation 1/2 − pmax. A negative value detects entanglement. Numerical results here apply to Bell-diagonal states only; the Python package supports general 2×2 and 2×3 density matrices with complex entries.

This page has no server calls, trackers, uploads, or data storage. It does not simulate measurements, estimate experimental uncertainty, or handle multipartite entanglement.
